How limits and credits work

Free plan: shared free pools

Free requests go to the free tiers of upstream providers (Groq, Google Gemini API, Cloudflare Workers AI, NVIDIA API Catalog and the free models on OpenRouter). Every Zero-X user shares those pools, and each pool has its own daily budget.

Free usage costs no credits but counts toward your daily request and token caps. When a shared pool runs out, requests fail with capacity_exhausted until it resets or another free model has capacity. Availability is best effort.

Pro and Max: paid capacity from credits

Paid plans use the free pools first, then switch to paid-capacity models when free capacity is unavailable. Paid-capacity requests are billed from your credit balance at the upstream provider's price plus 25%.

Pro includes $8 and Max includes $35 of credits at every monthly renewal. You can buy top-ups if you run low. The dashboard shows the cost of every request.

Questions

What counts as a request?

Each call to /v1/chat/completions is one request. Neuron makes one request per model turn, so a task that reads files, runs a command and then answers can use several requests. Requests that fail upstream after failover (upstream_error) are not counted.

What counts as a token?

Prompt tokens (what Neuron sends, including file contents and conversation history) plus completion tokens (what the model writes), as reported by the upstream provider. Both count toward your daily token cap. Long sessions grow the prompt; use /compact in Neuron to shrink it.

What happens when I reach a limit?

At the daily request or token cap, the gateway returns insufficient_quota with the reset time (00:00 UTC). Going over your per-minute rate returns rate_limited and Neuron retries after the delay. If a paid-capacity request needs more credits than you have, it returns insufficient_credits; top up or wait for your next renewal. Nothing is charged automatically beyond your plan.

Can I cancel? Do you give refunds?

Cancel any time from Billing in your dashboard. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for and does not renew. Refund terms are in the cancellation and refund policy.

Who sees my prompts and code?

Your requests are sent to the upstream provider that serves the selected model: Groq, Google Gemini API, Cloudflare Workers AI, NVIDIA API Catalog or OpenRouter (the free OpenRouter models Zero-X offers, or your own OpenRouter account if you connect one). Free tiers of some providers may use prompts to improve their models and allow human review, so do not send secrets or code you are not allowed to share on the Free plan. The privacy policy lists every processor.

Can I use my own API key (BYOK)?

Yes. Neuron can talk to any OpenAI-compatible endpoint with your own OPENAI_API_KEY and OPENAI_BASE_URL, and you can connect your own OpenRouter account. Requests sent with your own key are billed by that provider, not by Zero-X. See the configuration docs.

Do I need a card for the Free plan?

No. Install Neuron, run neuron login and approve the short code it shows while signed in to your Zero-X account.
