Neuron CLI plans

Start free. Upgrade when you need more.

The Free plan runs on shared free model pools with daily caps. Every plan includes Claude Haiku 5.5 at no charge, up to 10M tokens a month per account, inside the plan's limits. The paid plans, Athlete, Speed, Pro and Max, add Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Claude Opus 5.5, billed from the credits included with your plan, and raise the daily limits on the free models. Every plan has the same Neuron features.

Prices per month. No GST is charged, so the price shown is the price you pay. Payments are processed by Razorpay; your checkout shows the final amount before you pay.

How limits and credits work

Free plan: shared free pools

Free requests run on free models chosen for coding, with tool use, served from the free tiers of upstream services. Every Zero-X user shares those pools, and each pool has its own daily budget. The live model list shows which models are available right now. Free tiers may keep your requests, let people review them or use them to improve their models, so keep secrets and code you may not share out of Free-plan requests. The privacy policy lists who receives requests and what each provider states.

Free usage costs no credits but counts toward your daily request and token caps. When a shared pool runs out, requests fail with capacity_exhausted until it resets or another free model has capacity. Availability is best effort.

Paid plans: Claude from credits

Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Claude Opus 5.5 run when you choose them in Neuron (/model); auto stays on the free models. Each Claude request is billed from your credit balance at Anthropic's price plus 25%, using the token counts Anthropic reports, with cached prompt tokens at their lower rate. Daily request and token limits count every request, Claude included.

The paid plans add credits at every monthly renewal: Athlete $0.75, Speed $4, Pro $8 and Max $35. You can add $5 or $20 top-ups. When the free pools are busy, the paid plans can also use paid capacity of the free-tier models, billed the same way. The dashboard shows the cost of every request.

Claude Haiku 5.5 on every plan

Pick it with /model claude-haiku-5-5; auto stays on the free models. It costs no credits. Its tokens count toward your plan's request, token and per-minute limits like every free model, and you can use up to 10M of them a month. The allowance renews on the first of each month (UTC); when it is used up the request returns insufficient_quota with the renewal time, and every other model keeps working. Like the paid Claude models it has a daily spending limit shared by all users: when that is reached the model returns capacity_exhausted until 00:00 UTC.

Questions

What counts as a request?

Each call to /v1/chat/completions is one request. Neuron makes one request per model turn, so a task that reads files, runs a command and then answers can use several requests. Requests that fail upstream after failover (upstream_error) are not counted.

What counts as a token?

Prompt tokens (what Neuron sends, including file contents and conversation history) plus completion tokens (what the model writes), as reported by the upstream provider. Both count toward your daily token cap. Long sessions grow the prompt; use /compact in Neuron to shrink it.

How far does 3M tokens go?

Free counts input and output tokens on every model call, over a rolling 5 hours. Cached input, the part of the conversation the model has already seen, counts at 10%. A coding agent sends the conversation so far and the files it read with every step, and most of that is cached, so a typical Neuron step counts about 10,000 tokens and 3,000,000 tokens is roughly 300 such steps every 5 hours. The limit of 100 requests a day usually comes first. Use /compact in Neuron to shrink the prompt.

What happens when I reach a limit?

At the daily request or token cap, the gateway returns insufficient_quota with the reset time (00:00 UTC). On Free, tokens are counted over a rolling 5-hour window: once a window holds 3M tokens, insufficient_quota says when enough of it frees up (also in Retry-After), and tokens older than 5 hours stop counting. Going over your per-minute rate returns rate_limited and Neuron retries after the delay. If a paid-capacity request needs more credits than you have, it returns insufficient_credits; top up or wait for your next renewal. Nothing is charged automatically beyond your plan. A Claude request holds the most it could cost until it finishes, so with a low balance a request with a large max_tokens can return insufficient_credits; lower max_tokens or top up. Each Claude model also has a daily spending limit shared by all users; when it is reached the model returns capacity_exhausted until 00:00 UTC.

Can I cancel? Do you give refunds?

Cancel any time from Billing in your dashboard. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for and does not renew. Refund terms are in the cancellation and refund policy.

Do prices include GST?

No GST is charged: the price shown is the price you pay. For a card in another currency your bank may add its own conversion or card fee. Razorpay may email you a receipt, and your payments are listed in the Billing tab of your dashboard.

Who sees my prompts and code?

Your requests are sent to the upstream provider that serves the selected model, or the one auto picks: Anthropic (Claude Haiku 5.5 on every plan, Claude Sonnet 5.5 and Claude Opus 5.5 on the paid plans), or the free tier of an upstream provider for a free model. Anthropic does not train its models on requests sent through its API. Free tiers of some providers may use prompts to improve their models and allow human review, and for some providers we could not confirm their position, so do not send secrets or code you are not allowed to share on the Free plan. The gateway stores usage metadata only, not the text of your prompts, and replaces credentials it recognises with placeholders before a request leaves. The privacy policy has a table of who receives what and what each provider states; Data and subprocessors shows the data flow. With your own provider key, requests go straight to that provider.

Can I use my own API key (BYOK)?

Yes. Neuron can talk to any OpenAI-compatible endpoint with your own OPENAI_API_KEY and OPENAI_BASE_URL. Requests sent with your own key are billed by that provider, not by Zero-X. See the configuration docs.

Do I need a card for the Free plan?

No. Install Neuron, run neuron login and approve the short code it shows while signed in to your Zero-X account.
