Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026. Replaces the policy dated 25 October 2026. This version adds the billing details we ask for before a payment, the records of what you agreed to, Z.ai and Amazon Web Services as recipients, Microsoft and phone sign-in, phone notifications, a per-provider table, international transfers, cookies and local storage, a retention table, what happens to your data when an account is deleted, and a named grievance officer.

This policy covers the Zero-X website (zero-x.live and its subdomains), the account dashboard, the Zero-X gateway used by Neuron CLI, paid plans and the cloud features of NeuCockpit. Zero-X, a small business founded and run by Rahul S at 6/2, N. Pugalur, Karur 639113, Tamil Nadu, India, decides how this information is used.

Summary

  • We keep the account, usage, billing-detail and payment records needed to run the service and bill you.
  • When you use Neuron through Zero-X, the content of each request (your messages, file contents and command output) is sent to the model provider that serves it. If you choose auto, Zero-X picks the provider, so a request can go to any provider that is switched on at that moment. The table below lists them.
  • The gateway stores usage metadata only (time, model, token counts, cost and status). By design it does not write the text of your prompts, files or the model's responses to its database or logs. It holds that text in memory only while the request runs.
  • Free tiers of some providers may use prompts to improve their models and may let humans review them. For each provider the table says what the provider states, or that we could not confirm it. Do not send secrets or code you are not allowed to share on the Free plan.
  • Before a request leaves our gateway we replace credentials we recognise (API keys, tokens, private keys) with placeholders and put the real values back in the answer. This is a best-effort safeguard, not a guarantee.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not use your prompts or code to train models.

Information we collect

Account

You can sign in with Google, GitHub, Microsoft, an email link or a code we send by text message to your phone number; the sign-in screen shows the methods available at the time. We receive an account identifier and the profile information the method provides: email address, name and profile image when available, or your phone number. The Google sign-in integration does not request access to Gmail, Drive, Calendar or your contacts, and profile data is used only for account features; it is never added to model requests. We never receive your sign-in provider password. Text-message codes are sent through the messaging provider connected to our authentication service, which receives your phone number and the code.

Usage records

For every gateway request we record the time, model, provider, pool (free or paid), status, prompt and completion token counts (for Claude models, also how many prompt tokens were read from or written to Anthropic's prompt cache), credit cost, latency and a request ID, plus daily counters for your plan limits. These records power the dashboard, enforce limits, settle credits and help resolve billing questions.

CLI sign-in, sessions and API keys

When you run neuron login, we store the sign-in request so that you can check it came from your device before you approve it at neuron.zero-x.live/device. The request holds its short code, a hash of the device code, the Neuron version, and the device name and operating system that your computer reports. It also holds the IP address the request came from, with its approximate country, region, city and network. Codes expire after 10 minutes, and the request is deleted about a day after its code expires. Neuron CLI sessions are stored under SHA-256 hashes of their tokens. API keys are stored as SHA-256 hashes with their name, prefix, creation time and last-used time. We cannot recover a full key after it is shown to you once.

Billing details and what you agreed to

Before the payment window opens, Zero-X asks for your name, country, city and, if you are in India, your state. A postal code, street address and phone number are optional, and a business buyer also gives a business name and street address. We keep these details with your account so the next checkout can show them for you to review, and we use them for billing records, support and to meet legal obligations. You can change them at each checkout. We do not ask for a tax or GST number, and we never ask for card, UPI or bank details.

Each time you pay, we also record which version of the Terms of Service, the Privacy Policy, the cancellation and refund policy and (for subscriptions) the renewal notice you agreed to, whether it was a subscription or a top-up, and when. These records are append-only: nothing in our system edits or removes one while the account exists.

Razorpay's payment window is filled in with your name (the first 50 characters), email address and phone number only. Your address, city, state and postal code stay with Zero-X and are not sent to Razorpay.

Payments

Razorpay processes payments. We receive Razorpay customer, subscription and payment IDs, the plan, amount, currency, status and invoice links. When you pay we send Razorpay your Zero-X user ID and the plan or credit pack name so we can match the payment to your account. Card, UPI and bank details are entered in Razorpay's payment window and are not received or stored by Zero-X. When you start a payment, your browser loads Razorpay's checkout script from Razorpay, so Razorpay also sees your IP address and browser information.

Phone app and notifications

Neuron's phone remote control and notifications are being built and are not generally available yet. If you use them: we store a push token, the phone's platform and its name, and the list of your computers that share a session (name, public key and last-seen time). Notifications are delivered through Expo's push service and by Apple or Google, and say only that a session on a named computer needs you or has finished. They never contain session content. Remote-control traffic passes through Zero-X's relay encrypted end to end between your computer and your phone; Zero-X does not read it and does not store it.

Technical information

Our hosting providers process IP addresses, approximate country, browser and device information to deliver the site, prevent abuse and enforce rate limits. The caller's country also decides whether some free model pools can be used. Cloudflare's performance analytics beacon on some pages reports page and browser performance information to Cloudflare.

Support messages

If you email us, we keep the conversation to answer you and to handle refunds, disputes or legal requests.

Prompts, code and model responses

Neuron CLI runs on your computer and decides which file contents, command output and messages to include in each request. Those requests travel to the Zero-X gateway, which forwards them to an upstream model provider and returns the response.

Where requests run. Website and API traffic reaches us through Cloudflare. The gateway is built to run on Cloudflare Workers and on a server we operate on Amazon Web Services in Mumbai (ap-south-1). When the server handles a request, Cloudflare passes it there over a private tunnel, together with the caller's IP address and approximate location. Cloudflare and, when the server is used, Amazon Web Services therefore handle your request in transit, as well as the model provider.

The gateway stores usage metadata only. It records the usage records listed above and nothing else about a request. The text of prompts, files, command output and model responses is held in memory only while the request is forwarded, and by design is never written to our database or logs. Two exceptions let a multi-step conversation continue, and both stay in memory only: for Google Gemini models, the gateway keeps the opaque continuation tokens Gemini returns with tool calls (not your text) for up to 6 hours; for Claude models, it keeps Claude's replies (their reasoning summaries with Anthropic's signatures, their text and their tool calls) for up to 2 hours after the conversation last used them, so the next request in the conversation can send that reasoning back to Anthropic unchanged.

What our logs hold. Operational logs hold request IDs, model, provider, status and error codes, and a short error message from the provider when a request fails. We do not log prompts, files or responses on purpose, but a provider's error message could in rare cases quote a fragment of a request. Logs are kept for the periods in the retention table below.

Secrets. Before a request leaves the gateway, credentials found in messages, tool results and tool-call arguments (provider key formats, private keys, tokens, passwords in connection strings and values of secret-named settings) are replaced with placeholders, and the real values are put back in the answer. Detection favours precision, so it can miss a secret. Keep secrets out of what you send.

What providers do. The upstream provider processes the request under its own terms. Free tiers may use prompts and responses to improve their models and may allow human review. Paid plans can route requests to paid provider tiers when free capacity is unavailable. On Pro and Max, requests to Claude Sonnet 5.5 or Claude Opus 5.5 go to Anthropic. If you connect your own provider key directly in Neuron, those requests do not pass through Zero-X at all.

NeuCockpit's local search and local AI features process the documents you choose on your own computer. Its optional cloud AI features send the message and any included document text through the same gateway.

International transfers

Your data is processed in India (our server on Amazon Web Services, Razorpay and part of our traffic), in the United States (Anthropic, Groq, NVIDIA, OpenRouter and Google), in Singapore (Z.ai), and wherever Cloudflare and Supabase run their systems. We choose providers that publish their privacy terms and rely on the protections those providers state. You can avoid sending content to any of them by connecting your own provider key in Neuron.

When an account is deleted

You can ask us to delete your account (see Your rights). When we delete it, the account profile, usage, sessions, API keys, billing details and agreement records are deleted from our database, and so are our own copies of its credit ledger, subscription and payment records, because our database removes everything tied to an account together. Razorpay keeps its own record of every payment it processed, for as long as its policy and the law require, and we cannot delete that. Our database provider also keeps backups, and deleted data can remain in a backup until it expires. Model providers keep request data under their own policies, and we cannot delete it for you.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, complete, update or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to exercise your rights if you cannot. Residents of the EU, the UK and other regions have similar rights under their laws, including objecting to processing and data portability. Email support@zero-x.live from your account address with "Privacy request" in the subject. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We send a copy of your account data when you ask for access, and we respond and act on other requests within 30 days. There is no self-service button for these requests yet. You can change your billing details at your next checkout, sign out, revoke sessions and keys in the dashboard, and remove Zero-X from your sign-in provider's connected apps. We can tell you which providers handled your requests, because the model and provider are in your usage history.

Minimum age

Zero-X services are for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Cookies and local storage

We use one first-party cookie, shared across zero-x.live subdomains, to keep you signed in. It lasts up to 30 days and is removed when you sign out. In your browser's local storage we keep your price currency choice, a plan or pack you picked while you sign in (for 15 minutes), and a note that a payment was not yet confirmed, so the page does not let you pay twice (for 30 minutes). Session storage holds the Neuron device sign-in request while you approve it. We do not use advertising cookies. Cloudflare's performance beacon on some pages and the font and script providers listed above receive the usual browser information when a page loads.

Security

Provider API keys stay on the gateway and are never sent to Neuron CLI or the website. Sessions and API keys are stored as hashes, traffic uses HTTPS, and database access is limited to the gateway's service role. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at support@zero-x.live if you find a vulnerability.

Changes

We will publish updates here with a new effective date and, for significant changes, notify signed-in users by email or in the dashboard. If the Terms, Privacy Policy or refund policy change, we ask you to agree to the new version at your next checkout. A developer-focused view of the same information is in Data and subprocessors.
