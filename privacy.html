Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026. Replaces the policy dated 24 October 2026; this version states exactly what the gateway stores and for how long, and adds Anthropic, which serves the Claude models on Pro and Max.

This policy covers the Zero-X website (zero-x.live and its subdomains), the account dashboard, the Zero-X gateway used by Neuron CLI, paid plans and the cloud features of NeuCockpit. Zero-X, 6/2, N. Pugalur, Karur 639113, Tamil Nadu, India, decides how this information is used.

Summary

  • We keep the account, usage and payment records needed to run the service and bill you.
  • When you use Neuron through Zero-X, the content of each request is sent to the model provider that serves it. The gateway stores usage metadata only (time, model, token counts, cost and status). It does not store the text of your prompts, files or the model's responses.
  • Free tiers of some providers may use prompts to improve their models and may let humans review them. Do not send secrets or code you are not allowed to share on the Free plan.
  • We do not sell personal data and do not use your prompts or code to train models.

Information we collect

Account

When you sign in with Google, GitHub or an email link, we receive an account identifier and the profile information that method provides: email address, name and profile image when available. The Google sign-in integration does not request access to Gmail, Drive, Calendar or your contacts, and Google profile data is used only for account features; it is never added to model requests. We never receive your sign-in provider password.

Usage records

For every gateway request we record the time, model, provider, pool (free or paid), status, prompt and completion token counts (for Claude models, also how many prompt tokens were read from or written to Anthropic's prompt cache), credit cost, latency and a request ID, plus daily counters for your plan limits. These records power the dashboard, enforce limits, settle credits and help resolve billing questions.

CLI sign-in, sessions and API keys

When you run neuron login, we store the sign-in request so that you can check it came from your device before you approve it at neuron.zero-x.live/device. The request holds its short code, a hash of the device code, the Neuron version, and the device name and operating system that your computer reports. It also holds the IP address the request came from, with its approximate country, region, city and network. Codes expire after 10 minutes, and the request is deleted about a day after its code expires. Neuron CLI sessions are stored under SHA-256 hashes of their tokens. API keys are stored as SHA-256 hashes with their name, prefix, creation time and last-used time. We cannot recover a full key after it is shown to you once.

Payments

Razorpay processes payments. We receive Razorpay customer, subscription and payment IDs, the plan, amount, currency, status and invoice links. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay's checkout and are not received or stored by Zero-X.

Technical information

Our hosting provider processes IP addresses, approximate country, browser and device information to deliver the site, prevent abuse and enforce rate limits. The caller's country also decides whether some free model pools can be used (see below). Cloudflare's performance analytics beacon on some pages reports page and browser performance information to Cloudflare.

Support messages

If you email us, we keep the conversation to answer you and to handle refunds, disputes or legal requests.

Prompts, code and model responses

Neuron CLI runs on your computer and decides which file contents, command output and messages to include in each request. Those requests travel to the Zero-X gateway, which forwards them to an upstream model provider and returns the response.

The gateway stores usage metadata only. It records the usage records listed above and nothing else about a request. The text of prompts, files, command output and model responses is held in memory only while the request is forwarded, and is never written to our database or logs. Two exceptions let a multi-step conversation continue, and both stay in memory only: for Google Gemini models, the gateway keeps the opaque continuation tokens Gemini returns with tool calls (not your text) for up to 6 hours; for Claude models, it keeps Claude's replies (their reasoning summaries with Anthropic's signatures, their text and their tool calls) for up to 2 hours after the conversation last used them, so the next request in the conversation can send that reasoning back to Anthropic unchanged.

The upstream provider processes the request under its own terms. Free tiers may use prompts and responses to improve their models and may allow human review. Paid plans can route requests to paid provider tiers when free capacity is unavailable. On Pro and Max, requests to Claude Sonnet 5.5 or Claude Opus 5.5 go to Anthropic. If you connect your own provider key directly in Neuron, those requests do not pass through Zero-X at all.

NeuCockpit's local search and local AI features process the documents you choose on your own computer. Its optional cloud AI features send the message and any included document text through the same gateway.

How we use information

We use the information above to provide and secure the service, authenticate you, enforce plan limits and fair use, bill subscriptions and credits, answer support requests, prevent fraud and abuse, and meet legal obligations. We process it to perform our contract with you, with your consent where the law requires it, and for legitimate uses such as security. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Account, usage metadata and key records: while your account exists. After a verified deletion request we delete them within 30 days.
  • Prompt and response text: not stored. It exists on the gateway only in memory while a request is in flight (Gemini continuation tokens: up to 6 hours in memory; Claude's replies, kept to continue a conversation: up to 2 hours after last use, in memory).
  • Operational logs: request IDs, model, provider, status and error codes, never prompt or response text or keys. Kept for at most 7 days.
  • Device sign-in requests: the device name, IP address and approximate location shown on the approval page, deleted one day after the request expires (requests expire after 10 minutes).
  • Payment notifications from Razorpay: 90 days, used to process each payment event once.
  • Payment records: for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires, even after account deletion.
  • CLI sessions: the device name, operating system and app version a device reports at sign-in, and when it was last used. A session ends 30 days after its last use (at most 90 days after sign-in), when you sign the device out, or when you use "Sign out all CLI sessions". API keys last until you revoke them.
  • Phone push tokens: until you turn notifications off in the app, remove the phone, or the push service reports the app uninstalled. Notifications say only that a computer needs you or finished; they never contain your sessions.
  • Browser sign-in cookies: up to 30 days; signing out clears them.
  • Upstream providers: keep request data according to their own policies, for example for abuse monitoring.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, complete, update or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to exercise your rights if you cannot. Residents of the EU, the UK and other regions have similar rights under their laws, including objecting to processing and data portability. Email support@zero-x.live from your account address; we may ask you to confirm your identity and will respond within 30 days. You can also sign out, revoke sessions and keys in the dashboard, and remove Zero-X from your sign-in provider's connected apps.

Minimum age

Zero-X services are for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Provider API keys stay on the gateway and are never sent to Neuron CLI or the website. Sessions and API keys are stored as hashes, traffic uses HTTPS, and database access is limited to the gateway's service role. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at support@zero-x.live if you find a vulnerability.

We will publish updates here with a new effective date and, for significant changes, notify signed-in users by email or in the dashboard.
