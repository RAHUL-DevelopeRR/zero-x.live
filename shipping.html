Shipping and Delivery Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026. Replaces the policy dated 16 October 2026.

Digital service only

Zero-X sells access to an online service: Pro and Max subscriptions for the Neuron CLI gateway and credit top-up packs. We do not sell or ship physical goods, so there are no shipping charges, couriers or delivery addresses involved.

How and when you receive what you bought

  • Subscriptions: your account is upgraded as soon as Razorpay confirms the payment, normally within a minute. The new plan, limits and included credits appear in the dashboard and apply to Neuron CLI immediately.
  • Credit top-ups: credits are added to your balance as soon as the payment is confirmed.
  • Neuron software: free to download from GitHub Releases. The current release, 7.0.0-alpha.2, is a pre-release, so install it by version; see the install options. The older Neuron packages on npm and PyPI are not supported.

Razorpay emails a payment receipt to the address you used at checkout. Delivery is to your Zero-X account, so make sure you are signed in with the account you want upgraded.

Where the service is available

The service is delivered over the internet and can be used from most countries. Some upstream free model pools are not offered in certain regions (for example, the Gemini free pool is not available to callers in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland). The pricing page and documentation describe these limits.

If your purchase does not appear

If your plan or credits are not visible within 30 minutes of a successful payment, email support@zero-x.live with your account email and Razorpay payment ID. We reply within 2 business days and, if we cannot deliver what you paid for, refund it in full as described in the cancellation and refund policy.
