Cancellation and Refund Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026. Replaces the draft dated 16 October 2026.

This policy applies to Zero-X Pro and Max subscriptions and to credit top-up packs bought on zero-x.live. Payments are processed by Razorpay. The Free plan has no charges.

Cancelling a subscription

  • You can cancel at any time from the Billing tab of the dashboard, or by emailing support@zero-x.live from your account address.
  • Cancellation stops future renewals. Your plan and its daily limits stay active until the end of the billing period you already paid for, and then the account moves to the Free plan.
  • We do not charge cancellation fees. Cancelling does not by itself refund the current period; see below.

Refunds

New subscriptions

If you are not satisfied with a new Pro or Max subscription, you can ask for a full refund of your first payment within 7 days of that payment, as long as the subscription has not been used for paid-capacity requests (requests billed from credits). We cancel the subscription when we issue the refund.

Renewals

Renewal payments are not refundable once the new period has started, except where the law requires it or a charge was made in error. Cancel before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.

Credits

  • Credits that have been consumed by requests are not refundable.
  • An unused top-up pack can be refunded within 7 days of purchase if none of its credits have been used.
  • Included monthly credits are part of the subscription and are not refunded separately. Credits have no cash value and cannot be transferred.

Failed, duplicate or incorrect charges

If you were charged twice, charged after cancelling, or charged an amount different from the price shown at checkout, we refund the incorrect amount in full.

Accounts suspended for abuse

If we suspend an account for breaking the Terms of Service, for example by reselling access or bypassing limits, we do not refund the remaining period or consumed credits.

How to request a refund

  1. Email support@zero-x.live from the address on your Zero-X account.
  2. Include the Razorpay payment ID (it starts with pay_ and appears in your receipt and in the dashboard) and the reason for the request.
  3. We reply within 2 business days. Approved refunds are sent through Razorpay to the original payment method. Razorpay usually takes 5 to 7 business days to credit it, depending on your bank.
