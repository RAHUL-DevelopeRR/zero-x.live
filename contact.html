Grievance officer

Zero-X is a small business founded and run by Rahul S. He is the grievance officer for complaints about the service, your data or a payment: email support@zero-x.live with "Grievance" in the subject, or write to the postal address above. We acknowledge complaints within 2 business days and aim to resolve them within 30 days. See the privacy policy for where else you can complain.

Response times

  • We reply to every email within 2 business days (Monday to Friday, India Standard Time), usually sooner.
  • Payment problems, such as being charged without receiving your plan, are handled first.
  • Privacy and grievance requests are acknowledged within 2 business days and resolved within 30 days.

What to include

  • The email address on your Zero-X account.
  • For payments: the Razorpay payment ID (pay_…) from your receipt or the dashboard. Put "Refund request" in the subject for a refund.
  • For your data: "Privacy request" in the subject, and whether you want a copy of your data, a correction or deletion of your account (see the privacy policy).
  • For Neuron CLI problems: your operating system, neuron --version, how you installed it (install script, npm or from source), the output of neuron doctor, the command you ran and the x-request-id or error code you saw.

Never send passwords, session tokens, API keys or private source code by email. We will never ask for them.

You can also report Neuron bugs on GitHub issues. Report security problems privately to support@zero-x.live, not in a public issue.
