Questions

Before you ask

Anything else, email support@zero-x.live.

Do I own the code?

Yes. After the final payment we transfer the repository to your account, together with the accounts we set up for the project. You can keep working with us, hire someone else or run it yourself.

Who pays for hosting and the domain?

You do, in your own name, so you always control them. We set everything up for you, and renewals are billed to you directly by the provider.

What if I need changes later?

Take a care plan, or ask for one-off changes at ₹600 an hour. We tell you the hours before we start.

Do you charge GST?

No. Zero-X is not registered for GST, so the price shown is the price you pay. Prices are final, the same as on our Neuron CLI plans.

How do I pay?

By UPI, bank transfer or a Razorpay payment link. We issue an invoice for every payment.

What counts as a revision round?

One round is one combined list of changes to the agreed scope, sent after you review the staging link. We make all of them, then you review again. New pages or features outside the scope are priced as add-ons before we build them.
