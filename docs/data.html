Data and subprocessors

Where a Neuron request goes, what the Zero-X gateway stores and for how long, and which companies receive your data. The privacy policy is the binding statement; this page is the developer's view of the same facts. Last reviewed 3 November 2026.

Where a request goes

With the Zero-X gateway (after neuron login) a model request takes this path:

  1. Your computer. Neuron decides what to include: your message, the conversation so far, and the files or command output it read. You choose the folder, the permission mode and the commands.
  2. Cloudflare. The request reaches zero-x.live/v1 over HTTPS. Cloudflare sees it in transit and runs part of the gateway.
  3. Our own server, which may be hosted on Amazon Web Services in Mumbai (only when it serves the request, and the host can change). Cloudflare passes the request over a private tunnel, with the caller's IP address and approximate location.
  4. The gateway. It checks your session, plan and quota, replaces credentials it recognises with placeholders, keeps the text in memory only, and writes usage metadata to the database.
  5. Supabase. Our database holds the account, usage, session (as hashes) and billing records.
  6. The model provider. The one you chose, or the one auto picked among those switched on. The answer comes back the same way.

Other flows:

  • Payments. Your browser talks to Razorpay for card, UPI or bank details; Zero-X never receives them. Razorpay tells the gateway the payment ID, amount and status. Razorpay's window is filled in with your name, email address and phone number only.
  • Your own provider key. Neuron sends requests straight to that provider and Zero-X sees nothing.
  • Phone app (not generally available yet). Session frames are encrypted between your computer and phone and pass through Zero-X's relay unread and unstored. Notifications go through Expo and Apple or Google, carry a fixed message and the computer's name, and never session content.

What a token is

A token is a piece of text, roughly three-quarters of a word. Zero-X counts the tokens the provider reports: prompt tokens (everything Neuron sent, including history and files) plus completion tokens (what the model wrote). A coding agent resends its context with every step, so a typical Neuron step uses about 25,000 tokens, and the Free plan's 1,000,000 tokens per 5 hours is roughly 40 steps. Use /compact to shrink the prompt. Limits are on the pricing page.

Controlling where it goes

  • Your own key. Point Neuron at your provider with OPENAI_API_KEY and OPENAI_BASE_URL; the request never passes through Zero-X.
  • Training filter. If your client can add request headers, X-Neuron-Allow-Training: false keeps only offers Zero-X has recorded as not training on inputs. Providers whose position is unknown or yes are skipped, so fewer models are available.
  • Trust tiers. X-Neuron-Trust-Tiers: T0,T1 limits which provider tiers may see the prompt.
  • Keep secrets out. The gateway replaces credentials it recognises with placeholders before a request leaves, but detection can miss one. Neuron asks before it reads .env and credential files; see Security and trust.

Your data requests

Email support@zero-x.live from your account address with "Privacy request" in the subject, to get a copy of your account data, correct it or have your account deleted. We act within 30 days, and there is no self-service button yet. When an account is deleted, our database removes the usage, session, key, billing detail and agreement records, and also our own copy of its payment records; Razorpay keeps its own. Complaints go to the grievance officer, Rahul S, at the same address with "Grievance" in the subject; see the privacy policy.

Security

Provider API keys stay on the gateway. Sessions and API keys are stored only as SHA-256 hashes. Traffic uses HTTPS and database access is limited to the gateway's service role. To report a problem, see Security and trust.

Changes to this page

When a provider is added or removed, or a retention period changes, this page and the privacy policy change together, with a new effective date on the policy. 3 November 2026: Z.ai and Amazon Web Services (our own server, when in use) added, Gemini and Workers AI marked as switched off, billing details and agreement records added, retention table extended. 4 November 2026: an optional GSTIN or tax ID added to the billing details.
