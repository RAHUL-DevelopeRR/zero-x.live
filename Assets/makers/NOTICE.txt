Model maker logos

The SVG files in this folder are brand icons from Simple Icons 16.30.0 (https://simpleicons.org),
released under CC0 1.0 Universal (https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md).
Only the path data is kept; each file is named by the maker slug that GET /v1/models reports
(maker or owned_by): alibaba.svg is the "Alibaba Cloud" icon, moonshot.svg "Moonshot AI" and
mistral.svg "Mistral AI". The logos remain trademarks of their owners and are shown only to name
the company that makes each model. Zero-X is not affiliated with these companies. Makers without
a Simple Icons logo get a monogram badge drawn by the site (home.js and zx-tokens.css).
